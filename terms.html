Use information

Updated October 22, 2026.

Jesse Gmail CLI is a private, owner-operated desktop setup. It is not offered as a public service and accepts no customer registrations, subscriptions or payments.

This website describes that setup; it does not provide access to the owner's mailbox or permission to use the application. Google access remains subject to the owner's consent and Google's applicable terms and policies.

The owner reviews proposed actions. Outbound sending requires separate explicit approval and is disabled in the triage command. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

Privacy notice

Updated October 22, 2026. This notice describes the owner-only Jesse Gmail CLI setup.

Access and use

The tool uses Google OAuth to access the owner's email address, Gmail messages, headers, labels, drafts and requested attachments. Access supports mailbox review, unsent reply drafting and owner-requested organization. The Gmail modify permission is broader than the local command's permitted operations.

Storage and processing

OAuth tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. An OAuth client backup is stored in the owner's private 1Password vault. Email content, attachments and working outputs may be saved locally until the owner removes them.

When the owner chooses AI assistance, selected email content may be processed by the AI services the owner uses. Their handling and retention depend on the selected service and account settings. The local CLI does not train AI models, sell email data or use it for advertising.

Owner controls

The owner can revoke this application's access in Google Account connections, remove locally saved data, and manage retained AI conversations in the relevant services. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete existing local files or provider conversations.

This website

This static website contains only application information. It has no sign-in form, analytics script or mailbox connection. The hosting provider may process normal website request information under its own policies.

For questions about this private setup, use the support contact shown on its Google consent screen. It is not a public service and has no other registered users.

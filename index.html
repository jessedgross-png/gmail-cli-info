Jesse Gmail CLI

A private desktop tool used by its owner to review and organize a personal Gmail mailbox.

The tool searches and reads conversations and attachments, prepares unsent reply drafts, and applies owner-requested labels and archive/read changes. Sending is disabled in the triage command.

This is an information website, not a sign-in service. The application is not offered to other users. No mailbox content or credentials are published here.
